About Us

Where Movement
Meets Purpose

Everflow Sanctuary is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to cultivating a harmonious balance between nature, mindfulness, and community in the San Francisco Bay Area and beyond.

We bring people together through activities that foster environmental stewardship, spiritual connection, and social responsibility — from waterway cleanups to meditative walks, all woven into a single, transformative experience.

How It Works

The Everflow Experience

Every event combines three transformations into a single day in nature.

01

Move

Hit the trail. We hike, walk, or paddle through the Bay Area's most beautiful natural spaces — moving with intention, not just for exercise.

02

Restore

Clean as you go. Pick up trash, remove invasive plants, and actively restore the land and waterways you're moving through. Leave it better than you found it.

03

Flow

End in stillness. Close every event with group yoga, guided meditation, or breathwork — right there in nature where the work was done.

Every Everflow event. Every time.
